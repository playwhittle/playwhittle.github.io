HOW TO PLAY

Whittle a board of 64 countries down to the hidden one, in as few questions as you can.

  1. 1Pick a yes/no card. Countries that don't match get carved away. Is it in Europe? NO FrancePeruItaly JapanSpainKenya
  2. 2Swap weak cards. Not every card is a good cut. Tap the arrows on a card to trade it for a new one. Your first 2 swaps are free; after that, each swap counts as a question.
  3. 3Carve down to one, or guess. Think you know it? Tap Guess and pick a country. A guess counts as a question, and a wrong one counts as two. Earn the carving: Finish at or under par and the last block left is carved into a one-of-a-kind figure for your shelf. Go over par and it stays a rough block for good. At or under par Over par
